FPGA Software Engineer - Systematic Hedge Fund

Paragon Alpha · London, England · On-site · Full-time

About the job

We are seeking an experienced FPGA Software Engineer to join our systematic hedge fund. You will be responsible for designing and implementing high-performance FPGA systems for low-latency trading applications.

Responsibilities

Design and implement FPGA-based trading systems. Optimize existing FPGA designs for lower latency and higher throughput. Collaborate with quantitative researchers to implement trading strategies in hardware. Maintain and improve CI/CD pipelines for FPGA development.

Requirements

5+ years of FPGA development experience. Proficiency in VHDL and/or Verilog. Experience with Xilinx or Intel/Altera FPGAs. Knowledge of high-frequency trading systems. Strong understanding of network protocols (TCP/IP, UDP). Experience with low-latency system design.

Nice to Have

Experience with OpenCL or HLS. Knowledge of market microstructure. Background in systematic trading. Familiarity with Linux kernel bypass techniques.

What We Offer

Competitive base salary (£150k - £300k DOE). Annual bonus tied to fund performance. Comprehensive health insurance. Flexible working arrangements. Regular team lunches. Conference attendance and training budget.

About Paragon Alpha

Paragon Alpha is a systematic hedge fund based in London, specializing in high-frequency and algorithmic trading across global equity and derivatives markets. Our technology-first approach leverages cutting-edge hardware and software solutions to generate consistent alpha.

Application Process

Initial screening call with HR. Technical phone interview with senior engineers. Take-home FPGA design challenge. On-site interviews with the engineering and quant teams. Offer and references check.

Additional Information

This role requires UK work authorization. Relocation assistance available. Start date flexible. The role is based in our London office with optional remote work on Fridays.
